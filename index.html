N!TRO began as a student team and grew into a connected engineering programme: design, testing, enterprise and identity moving with one purpose.

  1. Sep 2025

    Team formation

    The first N!TRO structure takes shape, bringing technical and enterprise roles into one competition team.

  2. Oct 2025

    Digital identity launched

    The website and Instagram presence establish a public home for the project and its evolving identity.

  3. Dec 2025

    Design begins

    Research moves into initial CAD development, guided by regulations, airflow priorities and manufacturability.

  4. Jan 2026

    First design pass

    The car concept becomes a working system ready for structured review and iteration.

  5. Mar 2026

    Portfolio development

    Engineering decisions, project management and enterprise work begin their first documented pass.

  6. Apr 2026

    Manufacturing and test network

    Digital ideas become physical parts through foam machining, additive manufacturing and hands-on finishing, supported by specialist collaboration and external testing insight.

  7. 10 May 2026

    N!TRO places third in a field of 57 teams, earning a place at the India National Finals.

  8. 31 Jul to 2 Aug 2026

    India National Finals

    A tenth-place national finish from 93 teams, the Team Identity Award and a Best Pit Display nomination close a remarkable debut season.

  9. Aug 2026 onward

Front architecture

Parabolic ogive nose cone

A smooth, progressive nose profile designed to guide air around the body without abrupt changes in direction.

    One loop.
    Many disciplines.

    Every part travels through a shared cycle: question, model, test, make and review. Claims stay attached to evidence; ideas stay open to change.

    Airflow first

    Aerodynamics as a connected system.

    The nose, Halo Node, split sides, sidepods, underside, cut-outs and rear wing are reviewed together, because a local change can alter the flow experienced downstream.

    • Parabolic ogive development
    • Boundary transitions and attached flow
    • Underbody and side-flow management
    • Rear-wing concept development

    Model, review, repeat

    CAD built for decisions, not decoration.

    Concepts are iterated against competition regulations, aerodynamic intent, part interfaces and the realities of manufacturing before physical production begins.

    • Iterative geometry development
    • Regulation-aware packaging
    • Component interface reviews
    • Design-for-manufacture checks

    Evidence over assumption

    Multiple ways to challenge an idea.

    CFD and smoke-tunnel observations are used alongside external testing conversations. Work with BITS Pilani Formula Bharat brought ANSYS-based insight into nose and floor development.

    • CFD comparison
    • Smoke-tunnel observation
    • ANSYS collaboration
    • Physical fit and rolling checks

    Digital to physical

    Purpose-led process selection.

    The car combines a polyurethane foam body with PA12 Nylon components produced through SLS and MJF workflows. In-house prototypes accelerate fit checks before final manufacture.

    • Polyurethane foam body
    • SLS and MJF processes
    • PA12 Nylon functional parts
    • Bambu Lab P1S prototyping

    Small parts, system impact

    Wheels, bearings and Halo trials.

    Wheel geometry and bearing fit are developed as a system. Halo experiments compared translucent PLA with heavier resin trials, then moved through finishing and assembly reviews.

    • Triple-spoke wheel development
    • Bearing selection and fit
    • Translucent PLA Halo trials
    • Resin and finish comparison
